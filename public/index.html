Emergency

Broadcasts SOS with your last known position on the tactical channel, three times, and sirens every armed screen in the house. For real emergencies — everyone in range will hear it.

Activity

Detections tell you a frequency is busy — not what is being said. Voice transmissions have to be listened to, so tap Listen on any signal to hear it. Data modes are different: switch the receiver to APRS decode or Sensor monitor and those arrive here as actual readable messages.
Clearing the activity feed tidies the view only — the archive underneath keeps every decode and is what this searches.

Band monitor

Sweeps a band on a timer and logs anything new that appears, so you come back to a record of what was on the air while you were away. It only scans when nothing else is using the receiver, so it never interrupts listening.

NOAA weather radio

The seven NWS broadcast channels — tap to listen on the dashboard dial. The EAS decoder takes its turn in the receiver sweep automatically; real warnings push to mesh and siren alarm-mode devices.
